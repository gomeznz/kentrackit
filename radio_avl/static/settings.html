Map

Esri imagery works straight away but is lower resolution outside cities, and commercial use is under Esri's terms. LINZ aerial is sharper across NZ and licensed for commercial use: get a free key at basemaps.linz.govt.nz and enter it above or below.

Tiles are saved on the Pi as they're viewed. Pan around the operating area once while the Pi has internet and the map then works offline. Cached: –

Start view

Where the map opens before any unit has reported. Move the map below, then press Use this view.

Display

KAS-20 currently polls about every 19 s, so 60 s stale gives a couple of missed polls before a unit changes colour.

Users

Administrator: everything. Dispatcher: map, acknowledge alerts, edit zones, name units, reports and exports. Viewer: map and reports only (good for a wall display). Any user can be limited to certain units; they then only see those units everywhere.
