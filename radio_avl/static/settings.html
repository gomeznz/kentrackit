    Alerts

    Zone alerts are set per zone on the map's Zones tab.

    If the monitor radio's display shows any of these (comma-separated, any case), KenTrackIt raises an emergency: red banner and alarm until acknowledged. We still need a capture of a real emergency to confirm what the radio shows.

    Trip reports

    GPS positions wander by up to 30 m even when parked, so keep the radius above about 40 m.

    Open reports

    Voice recording

    Records every voice transmission from the monitor radio's audio output and files it in the transmission log, ready to play back from the map or the Channel page. Dispatchers and administrators can listen; every playback is written to the audit log. Plug the radio's receive audio (accessory or speaker output) into a USB sound card or K5TRA board on the Pi. It must be a different one from AirBridge's: a sound card can only be used by one program.

    Talk on a radio and aim for peaks around −12 dB (the meter's yellow band). Turn the radio's volume or the sound card's input gain (alsamixer) up or down to suit.

    Front-panel display

    Shows who's on air, the last caller, units heard and alerts on a 20×4 character LCD with an I2C backpack (the common PCF8574 board). Wire it to the Pi: GND → pin 6, VCC → pin 2 (5 V), SDA → pin 3, SCL → pin 5. Turn I2C on first with sudo raspi-config (Interface Options → I2C). An emergency takes over the screen and flashes the backlight.

    Polling

    KenTrackIt can ask units for their positions itself, instead of KAS-20: moving units often, parked units rarely, units in an emergency constantly, and always waiting for a clear channel. Missed polls go onto the coverage map.

    Live polling transmits on the customer's channel. Switch KAS-20 off first, so units aren't polled by both.

    Diagnostics: raw serial capture

    Records exactly what the radio sends (and what KenTrackIt sends to it) while the service keeps running. Use it to capture a voice call, a status message, an emergency or a KAS-20 poll so they can be decoded. Files replay with --replay.
